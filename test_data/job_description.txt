TALENTA PARTNERS GROUP — JOB POSTING
Role: HR Manager (مدير الموارد البشرية)
(Translated / organized from the company's original Arabic posting)

ABOUT THE COMPANY
Talenta Partners Group specializes in recruitment through direct search and
by providing HR consultants. They support clients in acquiring top-level
talent and in matching candidates whose profiles align with their clients'
professional projects, helping clients find the best job opportunities.

ROLE SUMMARY
As part of our search work, we are recruiting an HR Manager for one of
our client companies.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Ensure administrative organization of employees across the full employee
  lifecycle (hiring, contracts, amendments, departures, etc.)
- Ensure administrative files comply with legal/regulatory requirements
- Monitor workforce KPIs and productivity indicators
- Master payroll management processes, coordinating with relevant
  stakeholders
- Control variable payroll elements
- Monitor time tracking, absences, leave, and overtime
- Ensure the reliability of workforce and payroll data
- Participate in recruitment campaigns
- Write and distribute job postings
- Manage sourcing, screening, and interview coordination for open roles
- Contribute to onboarding of new employees
- Participate in training-needs engineering
- Identify training needs in coordination with department managers
- Develop the training plan and follow up on its execution
- Ensure monitoring and evaluation of training procedures

REQUIREMENTS
- Bachelor's degree or higher in Human Resources / Management + 5 years
  of experience
- Proven experience in a general (generalist) HR role
